History of Economic Thought

A course of twenty-five topics from mercantilism to the economics of the twenty-first century, following each school of thought through the historical conditions that produced it.

  • Topic-by-topic outline with historical context and readings

The Sixties Generation

Profiles of the economists and social scientists shaped by decolonization and the revolutions of the 1960s, who rethought development, empire, and liberation from the standpoint of the world economy’s periphery.

  • Forerunners of the Monthly Review circle: Baran, Sweezy, and Magdoff
  • Frank, Amin, Emmanuel, Wallerstein, Arrighi, dos Santos, and Rodney
  • The American wing: Hymer, Bowles and Gintis, Weisskopf, and the founding of URPE
  • Their lives, their work, and a short chronology, in English and Chinese
